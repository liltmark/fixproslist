The FixProsList Partner System: daily posting and real customer calls

The Partner System is a posting and leads system for home service contractors. It is run by FixProsList. The system puts your business in front of customers every day and sends the calls to your phone. You do the work on site. We run the online side for you.

It is built for plumbers and electricians and roofers and every other home service trade. You get your own login. You get your own dashboard. And you see every call the system brings you.

First month free trial. You pay only after you get paid. No card attached. Nothing in advance.

What you get as a member

  • Your own login and personal dashboard. You sign in and see your own posts and your own calls. You never see another member and another member never sees you.
  • Connect your own accounts. Your own Facebook and your other accounts connect to the system. The posting goes out from your own pages and your own name.
  • Daily posting done through the system. Videos and posts and Marketplace listings are prepared and posted for your business every day. Your business stays active online even on the days you are busy on site.
  • Every call tracked and recorded. Each call lands in your dashboard with the date and the time and the length. The recording is there too. Nothing is hidden from you.
  • Leads from our websites and listings. Customers also find you through FixProsList and our partner sites. Your listing on FixProsList is free forever.

What it costs — stated openly

There is one money rule in this system and it is the same for every member.

  • Your listing is free. Joining is free. There is no setup fee and no monthly fee.
  • You pay only for billable calls. A billable call is a real customer in your area with a real job. The exact rules are written on the Partner Rules page.
  • One flat rate: $80 per qualified call. The same rate for every trade. No tiers and no surprises.
  • Nothing is paid in advance. Calls come first. Payment comes after.
  • Settled weekly. You see every charged call in your dashboard before the week is settled.
No calls means no cost. If the system brings you no qualified calls in a week then you pay nothing for that week. The listing stays free either way.

How to join

  1. 1. Create your free account. Sign up on the partner page with a simple email code. There is no password to remember.
  2. 2. Add your business listing. Tell us your trade and your city and your phone number. Our team reviews every listing by hand.
  3. 3. Get your system login. Once your listing is approved we set up your place in the system and send you your login.
  4. 4. Connect your accounts and start. You connect your own Facebook and other accounts. Daily posting begins and the calls come to your phone.
