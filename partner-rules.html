Direct Calls: pay only when a real customer calls

Direct Calls is FixProsList's pay-per-call program for home-service pros. Your listing is free forever. We put our own tracked phone number on it, calls ring straight to your phone, and you pay one flat rate — $80 — only for qualified calls. Nothing in advance. No contract, no monthly fee, no membership. If the calls don't come, you pay nothing.

These rules are the whole agreement in plain English. They are shown to every partner before the first call, and no charge can ever fall outside them.

How it works

  1. 1. Create your free account and submit your business listing — it takes a few minutes, with a simple email code (no password).
  2. 2. We review and attach your call number. We check your listing by hand, approve it, and place your tracked FixProsList number on it. The listing goes live on fixproslist.com.
  3. 3. Your number activates. Nothing is paid in advance — no balance to load. Calls ring your own phone — you answer and book the job like any other call.
  4. 4. Pay only for qualified calls. Each qualified call is charged at the one flat rate — $80 — and charges are settled weekly. Every call, its length and its recording appear in your Partner Dashboard.

What counts as a billable call

A call is charged only if all four are true:

  • Long enough: the call lasts at least your trade's minimum (90 seconds for most trades, 60 seconds for appliance repair), counted from when you — or your voicemail or answering service — pick up.
  • New customer: the caller is not an existing customer calling back about a job already in progress.
  • In your service area: the job is inside the ZIP codes or radius you chose on your listing.
  • Real service intent: a homeowner or property manager asking about a real job in your trade — a quote, a repair, a booking.

Never charged, ever

  • Spam, robocalls, telemarketers and bots
  • Wrong numbers and misdials
  • Job seekers, sales calls, and other contractors fishing
  • Duplicate calls from the same person about the same job within 7 days
  • Calls under the minimum length, hang-ups, and unanswered calls
The recording is the proof. Every call is recorded, and the recording plus the call log are the only evidence used in any billing question. If you dispute a charge, we play the recording together; if the call doesn't meet the rules above, the charge is reversed — no forms, no fight.

How payment works

  • Nothing in advance. No setup fee, no membership, no monthly bill, no balance to load. Your tracked number is assigned when your listing is approved.
  • Pay after the calls. Each qualified call is charged at the flat $80 rate and charges are settled weekly. Every charge shows in your dashboard with the date, time, duration — and the recording.
  • Only qualified calls are charged — exactly the rules on this page. Spam, duplicates, wrong numbers and short calls are always free.
  • Leave any time. No cancellation fee, no notice period — you owe nothing beyond the qualified calls already delivered.

Call recording notice

Every call to a FixProsList tracked number opens with a short recorded announcement: "This call may be recorded for quality and billing verification." The recording protects you as much as it protects us — it is the shared, checkable record of what was actually said, and it is how billing disputes are settled fairly.

Direct Calls is pay-as-you-go — you pay only after real customer calls come in. There is no term contract, no minimum monthly spend, and no fee to join or to leave. Your listing stays free forever whether or not you use Direct Calls.
